# KlimateX demo video: narration script

83 seconds, 1920x1080, H.264 + AAC. The voice is the macOS system voice "Daniel"; re-record over it with a team member's voice if you prefer. Captions are in `klimatex-demo.vtt`. The video file itself is in `web/public/demo/klimatex-demo.mp4` (the landing page serves it from there).

| # | Screen | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Title | KlimateX turns a Lagos family's switch from a petrol generator to solar into verified carbon credits, and pays the family their share. |
| 2 | Landing page | It is one platform, with a dashboard for everyone in the chain. Here they are. |
| 3 | Phone: sign-in, home, loan | Families use the phone app. Adaeze signs in with her phone number and a PIN. She sees her grade, the petrol she saved, and what she has been paid, straight into her Access Bank solar loan. |
| 4 | Phone: earn more, fuel receipts, assistant | The app tells her how to earn more, from her own readings: add last year's fuel receipts, and run the backup generator less. She can ask questions by typing or by voice, in five languages. |
| 5 | Phone: how it's calculated, certificate, payouts | Every figure can be checked: the working for each month, and a home energy certificate. |
| 6 | Household dashboard (web) | The same household dashboard runs on the web. |
| 7 | Installer dashboard | Installers see every home they fitted, and which ones need attention. |
| 8 | Auditor portal | Auditors review the evidence, sample homes, and sign off each period. Nine checks run on every home's readings. |
| 9 | Credit marketplace | Companies buy and retire the credits in the marketplace. Half of every sale goes to the family. |
| 10 | Lender dashboard | Access Bank sees its solar loans by measured energy grade. |
| 11 | Operations console | Our operations team sees every home, payout and loan in one view. |
| 12 | Street grades | And anyone can see how clean their street's power is, or check a credit's serial. |
| 13 | Closing | KlimateX. Measured, audited, and paid. |

All screens were captured from a local copy of the product running in mock mode. Data and households are synthetic.
